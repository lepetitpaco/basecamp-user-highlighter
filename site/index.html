A browser extension that lights up Basecamp cards and to-dos in each person's color.

Download for Chrome / Edge Download for Firefox

Chrome Web Store and Firefox Add-ons listings are on their way.

What it does

Everything is set from the extension popup and applies right away.

One color per person

On card tables, to-do lists and My Tasks. A shared task shows all its colors.

One-click add

People you come across in Basecamp show up in the popup on their own. Or right-click an avatar.

Easy to read

The tint goes under the text, and nothing lights up inside a card or a to-do.

Your way

Color picker, intensity, dim everyone else's tasks, or just a border.

FAQ

Do my teammates see the colors?

No. The highlighting only exists in your browser, and nothing changes on your Basecamp account.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere: everything stays in the extension's storage on your device. See the privacy policy.

Something broke after a Basecamp update.

The extension reads Basecamp's pages, so a redesign can break it. Please open an issue.
