Basecamp User Highlighter

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Summary

Basecamp User Highlighter does not collect, sell, share or transmit any personal data. It has no servers, no analytics and no tracking. Everything it saves stays in your browser.

What is stored

In the extension's local storage, on your device:

  • Highlighted people: Basecamp id, display name, color, on or off.
  • People you come across on the Basecamp pages you open: id, name and avatar address, to offer them in the quick add.
  • Your settings: intensity and display options.

Network access

The extension makes no requests of its own, apart from its popup showing avatars hosted by Basecamp. It doesn't use Basecamp's API and changes nothing on your account. It only runs on app.basecamp.com and 3.basecamp.com.

Permissions

  • storage: to save people and settings.
  • contextMenus: the right-click entry on avatars.
  • scripting: to start the extension in Basecamp tabs already open after an install or an update.
  • Access to app.basecamp.com and 3.basecamp.com: to highlight tasks on those pages.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes all its storage.
